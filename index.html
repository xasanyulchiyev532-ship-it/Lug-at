Lesson 28 – Size

Big, large, small, little, tall, high — qachon qaysi so‘z ishlatiladi?

1. Big vs Large

Big kundalik va norasmiy nutqda ko‘proq ishlatiladi. Large biroz rasmiyroq, lekin ko‘p hollarda ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi.

  • Large odatda statistikalar va o‘lchovlar bilan keladi: large number, large amount, large quantity, large proportion, large increase.
  • Ichimlik va kiyim o‘lchamlari: a large coffee, size large.
  • Large-scale / small-scale — faoliyat miqyosi uchun.

Lekin quyidagi hollarda faqat big ishlatiladi (large bo‘lmaydi):

  • big decision, big mistake, big surprise, big disappointment, big failure, big improvement, big accomplishment
  • big brother / big sister (aka-uka, opa-singil)

2. Small vs Little

Small — neytral

Jismoniy o‘lchamni bildiradi, his-tuyg‘usiz.

• a small house
• small children
• small dog
• small businesses

Little — his-tuyg‘u bilan

Mehribonlik, achinish yoki yoqtirishni bildiradi. Ko‘pincha cute, pretty, poor, nice, tiny bilan keladi.

• a cute little house
• pretty little girls
• poor little dog

Little miqdorni ham bildiradi (“ozgina”) — bu yerda small ishlatilmaydi:

  • a little money (ozgina pul)
  • little salt (ozgina tuz)
  • slept very little (juda kam uxladim)
  • little information (ozgina ma’lumot)

3. Tall / Short vs High / Low

Tall / Short

Jismoniy narsaning pastki qismidan yuqori qismigacha bo‘lgan balandligi.

• tall building
• tall trees
• tall man / short woman

High / Low

Yerdan qanchalik balandda joylashganligi yoki “daraja”.

• flying high
• low branch
• high/low prices, levels, quality, rate, degree

4. Scale

Large-scale — keng miqyosdagi, katta hajmdagi faoliyat.
Small-scale — kichik miqyosdagi, cheklangan hajmdagi faoliyat.

  • large-scale production — keng miqyosdagi ishlab chiqarish
  • small-scale farming — kichik miqyosdagi dehqonchilik
